WHY SONORA EXISTS

A diary you can listen to.

Some days are easier to remember through a song than a sentence. SONORA is a home for those moments: save what you were listening to, write what it meant, and give that feeling a colour. Over time, your songs become constellations — a quiet map of who you were, and who you are becoming.
